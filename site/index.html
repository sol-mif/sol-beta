Before you open it.

Free while it's in beta. You only pay for the AI you already have. During the beta Sol counts which buttons you press, how long things take and what broke, with your name on the counts. Never what you type, say, read or open: the privacy page says exactly what.

A Mac

Apple silicon or Intel, on macOS 14 or newer. Linux runs from the one line, with the window in your browser; Windows and the phone come later.

Your own ChatGPT or Claude plan

ChatGPT Plus or Pro, or Claude Pro or Max. Either one works; both is best. You sign in yourself, so Sol never sees your password and the thinking runs on your own plan.

Fifteen minutes

Download, open, say your name, allow a few things, sign in. Something odd? Tell us on Discord; the three of us who build Sol answer, usually the same day.

  1. Open Sol. macOS says "Sol" Not Opened. Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway.
  3. Click Open Anyway once more and type your Mac's password. Sol opens, and it never asks again.

Apple registers apps for a yearly fee, and we haven't done that yet for the beta, so your Mac is careful the first time. Nothing else is different.
