# Sol: questions and answers

The words Help answers from, in plain words, for anyone using Sol. Each question is a `##` line and its answer is the
paragraph under it. A `Show me:` line names the walk and step (from 0) that shows the answer on your own Sol.

## What does "Sol knows 12" mean?
Sol has read 12 things in this folder (notes, files, chats), so a session here starts already knowing them.
Show me: ai 0

## What's a seat?
An AI plan you're signed into, like Claude or Codex. "1 coming up" means one is still signing in.
Show me: ai 3

## What do "only looks", "prepares" and "does it" mean?
How much Sol may do in a place: read only, get things ready for your OK, or act on its own.
Show me: yours 2

## What is "What every session here knows"?
The folder's notes every new session reads first. Edit them and every session after uses the change.

## What does Unfold do?
It shows what's inside a stack of folders, card by card. Press again to stack them back.

## What are MCP tools?
Extra abilities your AI can use, like reading your calendar. You add them in Claude or Codex, and Sol shows the ones you have.

## What's the digest?
A short summary of what happened while you were away, at the top of the Diary.
Show me: needs 1

## Why does the AI ask first?
Asks first is on for that session. Switch it in the model chip, under "Before acting".
Show me: ai 3

## Is anything deleted when I tidy?
No. The Shelf and Arrange only move things, and Undo brings them back.
Show me: folders 3

## What can Sol see?
Only what you let it, place by place, in How Sol sees you. Turn a source off and Sol forgets it; Back brings it back.
Show me: yours 2
