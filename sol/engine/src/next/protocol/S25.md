### Replay readings (Harmony S25, #1617; `next/reading.ts`)

Behind `ui.next`, answered only to Sol's own windows (anything else hears `next.refused`). Read only: it reads the Diary and the needs and writes nothing.

- `next.replay {span: "night"|"week"|"month-fits"}` (default `night`) → `next.replay {span, from, to, stops: [{place, at, outcome, ms}], rest}` to the asker.
  - Spans, in the Mac's local time: `night` runs from 18:00 to 09:00 the next day, or to now. `week` is the last 7 days and `month-fits` the last 30, with only the fit acts kept.
  - Order: each place's best `next/needs.ts` rank among the needs open on it (best first), then its count of acts in the span (most first), then its name.
  - Time: 60 s at most in all. Each stop gets 2 to 15 s, and the rest of the minute is shared by count of acts. Places that don't fit at 2 s each are left out, and `rest` counts them.
  - `outcome`: "waits on you" when an ask is open there. Otherwise it's the last outcome there (a run's end, or a pace wait), or else the last act's kind.

Live acts are the Diary's lines in the span. A line's `region` is its place, and a line with none goes under `diary`. A line carrying `embed.pace` is a run that waits (S20). A line carrying `embed.fit` is a fit. Until S15's fit store is read here, `month-fits` holds only those lines.
