### fits

From Harmony slice S15 (`engine/src/next/fits.ts`; answered by `parts/next-s15.ts`). Behind `ui.next`: off, nothing is
answered and nothing is read or written. Only Sol's own windows are answered; any other connection hears
`{ type, ok: false, why: "only Sol's own windows" }` and nothing is read. A fit is a `take` on a `knob` in a `scope` (a
place, a folder path: `"dev"`, `"dev/api"`). Proposed for BR1, the engine lead's word (#1615): `"~"` is All of Sol, above every
folder (`"~/x"` is refused), and `"session:<id>"` is one session, read before the folder it works in. Every message here is the person's; Sol's own writes and offers go through
`fits.ts` (`solSet`, `offer`), never over the wire.
- `next.fits { scope?, folder? }` → `next.fits { ok: true, fits: [{ knob, scope, take, author, reason, count, source, change, held, history }] }`.
  With a scope, the fits that bear on it: its own and those of every scope above it, up to `"~"`; for a
  `session:<id>` scope, `folder` (that session's folder) is read under it. `author` is `"person"`
  (customization), `"sol"` (personalization) or `"default"` (nothing standing: `take` null). `reason` and `count` are the
  standing take's (every take Sol writes has both: the count is from the person's history). `held` is true after one
  Back by the person, until they let it go. `history` is every step on that knob in that scope, oldest first:
  `{ id, at, act: "set" | "back" | "release" | "offer" | "keep" | "notnow", take?, author?, reason?, count?, source?,
  undoes?, undone? }` (`id` is the step's change id, `undoes` the change a Back took back, `undone` on a set a Back took
  back). `change` is the change id of the take standing (null when none), `source` where it came from (null when
  unknown: never guessed). The take a knob has in a scope is the nearest scope's with a take standing; a
  scope held with nothing standing stops that walk (what was taken back is not handed back from above), and a knob held
  in a scope is held in every scope under it.
- `next.fit.set { knob, scope, take, source? }` → `next.fit.set { ok: true, fit }` or `{ ok: false, why }`: the person's take.
  `{ knob, scope, held: false }` lets Sol fit that knob there again (`why: "it is not held"` when it is not).
- `next.fit.back { knob, scope }` → `next.fit.back { ok: true, fit }` or `{ ok: false, why: "nothing set there to take
  back" }`: the take standing in that very scope is taken back by a new event (never an edit), the one before it returns,
  and the fit is held. While held, or while the take is the person's, Sol neither writes nor offers there. After one
  "not now" Sol never offers that knob in that scope again; while an offer is open, it makes no second.

Kept in `SOL_HOME/next/fits.json` `{ v: 1, events }`, one event per act, never edited, so any past state replays
(`fitsAt(home, t)`). The events have the Home log's shape (`kind`, `knob`/`scope` for `item`, `cause`); moving them onto
the log needs two rows in `contract/kinds.ts` (`fit.set`, `fit.back`), which this slice does not own.
