### context

From Harmony slice S29 (`engine/src/next/context.ts`; answered by `parts/next-s29.ts`). Behind `ui.next`: off, nothing is
answered and nothing is read or written. Only Sol's own windows are answered; any other connection hears
`{ type, ok: false, why: "only Sol's own windows" }` and nothing is read. A fact belongs to a place (a folder path:
`"denise"`, `"denise/bookings"`), and every fact has a source, or it is not kept.
- `next.context { scope }` → `next.context { ok: true, scope, facts, proposals }`. `facts`: the facts of that place and of
  every place above it, nearest place first, then oldest first: `{ id, place, fact, source, learned, stale, staleWhy?,
  offLimits?, by, seat?, at }`. `learned` is true for every fact the person did not say (Sol's own, or a seat's they
  kept). `stale` is true when a newer fact replaces it, or a check found its ground gone; a stale fact still shows, with
  `staleWhy`. A removed fact is not in it. `proposals`: the seats' proposals still open there, `{ id, place, fact, source,
  seats, count, at, replaces? }`.
- `next.fact.add { place, fact, source, replaces?, offLimits? }` → `{ ok: true, fact, again? }` or `{ ok: false, why }`: the
  person's own fact. `replaces` is the id or the words of a fact in that same place, which is stale from now (and stays stale if the newer
  one is later removed). The same words
  standing there already are that fact (`again: true`).
- `next.fact.propose { place, fact, source, seat, replaces? }` → `{ ok: true, proposal, ask: "Keep this?" }` or
  `{ ok: false, why }`: a seat's learning, never a fact until kept. The same words proposed again in that place while
  open land on the open proposal (`count` and `seats` grow). `why`: `"it is a fact here already"`, or removed (below).
- `next.fact.keep { id, keep }` → `{ ok: true, proposal, fact? }`: kept, the proposal becomes a learned fact with its
  source (or the same words the person already said there, never a second); `keep: false` is kept as a no, and no seat
  nor Sol puts those words to them again in that place (the person may still add them).
- `next.fact.remove { id, undo? }` → `{ ok: true, fact, again? }`: a hold, never a delete. The fact stays in the file, out
  of every context, and nobody may add or propose its words in that place again until `undo: true`.
